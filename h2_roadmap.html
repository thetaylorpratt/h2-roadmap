Canvas · Discovery Domain · H2 2026

Where Canvas is going in the second half of 2026.

Canvas shipped at GA as an investigator: it starts working the moment something looks wrong, building the case while you're still waking up. Over the next six months we build toward something more: a strategic partner that connects what's happening in your systems to what it means for your business. This is the path.

Today · shipped at GA

The investigator

Alert-triggered investigations, evidence stacks, agentic chat, multiplayer collaboration, read-only GitHub. The do-er that builds the case while you're still waking up.

→
Building toward · H2

The strategic partner

Translates incidents into business impact, spots instrumentation gaps, tailors onboarding to your stack. The strategist that sees what you don't see yet.

Internally we call these two jobs Ada (the investigator) and Atlas (the strategist). The names are just shorthand. What matters is the jobs they do, and most of the work below is the foundation that gets us from one to the other.
